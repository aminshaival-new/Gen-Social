# Creative Threads — catalogue reference

## Style reference (catalogue-style-reference.jpg)
- Full-bleed hero photo, heavily blurred, as the page background
- Centered cream card (#FBF7EC-ish), thin white border, landscape split ~45/55
- Left panel: brand label top-left (serif caps), small detail crop centered,
  product name in serif caps, fabric breakdown in small sans below
- Right panel: full-length styled model shot
- Palette pulled from the photography (sage green, blush pink, cream)
- Quiet, editorial, lots of negative space

## Brand logo (creative-threads-logo.png)
- Needle + swirling thread monogram, "CREATIVE THREADS" in condensed display caps
- Gradient: deep royal blue (#1A1FA8-ish) to cyan (#0AD0F5-ish)
- Note: logo blue clashes with the soft blush/sage reference palette — needs a
  mono/white or single-blue logo variant on catalogue pages
